# Music Fan

Music Fan is an Android music player designed primarily for local music and personal, self-hosted music libraries.

It brings music stored on your device, personal servers, and network storage into a single playback experience. In addition to local music, Music Fan currently supports sources such as Navidrome and WebDAV, along with features such as a unified music library, playback queue, lyrics, and offline playback.

## Features

### Music Library

- Local music scanning and management
- Personal music server support
- Navidrome support
- WebDAV support
- Support for multiple music sources
- No dependency on third-party online music platforms for music content

### Playback

- Playback queue
- Shuffle
- Repeat
- Lyrics
- Offline playback
- Background playback
- Lock screen and system media controls

### Interface

- Built with Jetpack Compose
- Designed for both phones and tablets
- Adaptive layouts for different screen sizes
- Based on Material 3, with design ideas inspired by other platforms
- Custom-designed interfaces for the player, music library, song lists, and other parts of the app

## Music Sources

Music Fan is primarily designed for managing and playing your own music collection.

Local music can be scanned directly from folders on your device. Music stored on a personal server can also be accessed through sources such as Navidrome and WebDAV.

The project does not provide or bundle unauthorized commercial music, nor does it rely on third-party online music platforms as its primary source of music.

## Development Status

Music Fan is currently in active development.

Development focuses on improving areas such as:

- Playback experience
- Music library management
- Network-based music sources
- Lyrics
- Playback queue
- Phone and tablet support
- Animations and interaction details
- Performance and stability

Some features may still have known issues. If you encounter a problem, feel free to report it through GitHub Issues.

## About

Music Fan is a personal Android music player project.

It is not intended to be a replacement for any particular commercial music player. The project exists to build a music player that better fits its own usage needs.

The project puts particular emphasis on local music, self-hosted music libraries, playback interactions, and the overall experience across different devices.
